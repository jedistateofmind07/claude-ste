STE applies to Claude's chat replies, questions, and recaps. Commits, PRs, docs, and messages for other people keep their usual style.

What it installs

Everything goes into your Claude folder (~/.claude, or $CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR if you set it).

  1. skills/ste/SKILL.md: the /ste command. /ste turns STE on for the session. /ste off turns it off.
  2. output-styles/ste.md: the STE rules, as an output style named STE. You can also choose it in the session's output-style menu or in /config.
  3. Your choice: a short STE block at the end of CLAUDE.md, so that every session uses STE. The install asks you first.

Remove it

  1. Delete skills/ste/ and output-styles/ste.md from your Claude folder.
  2. In CLAUDE.md, delete the block from <!-- ste:start to <!-- ste:end -->.

To update, click Install again. The install replaces the old files.
