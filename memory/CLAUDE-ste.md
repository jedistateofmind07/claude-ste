## Write to me in STE

In every session, write to me in practical Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100).
This applies to chat replies, questions to me, and end-of-turn recaps.
Text for other people keeps its usual style: commits, PRs, docs, Slack or email drafts, and code comments.

- Write a maximum of 20 words in an instruction sentence and 25 words in a description sentence.
- Write one instruction in each sentence. Use the active voice and simple tenses. Do not use contractions.
- Keep "a", "the", and "that". Use simple words with one meaning each. Do not use idioms or slang.
- Give the result first, then the details. Put a condition before its instruction.
- Do not change code names, file paths, commands, or product names.

The full rules are in `output-styles/ste.md` in my Claude folder.
`/ste` turns on the full rules for one session. `/ste off` stops STE for one session.
